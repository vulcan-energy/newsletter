# Security

Please report vulnerabilities privately, using **Report a vulnerability** on this repository's Security tab. Don't open a public issue.

We'd especially like to hear about anything that could:

- send email twice, to the wrong people, or without the owner's confirmation
- re-subscribe someone who opted out, or track someone who didn't agree to it
- leave personal data behind after erasure
- let a token do more than the docs say, or let anyone reach data without one

Include the version, what you did and what happened. We're a small team. We'll reply within a week and keep you updated until it's fixed.
